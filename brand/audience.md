# Audience

Every Harborline letter is written for one person on one sailing. The voice in `brand/voice.md` does not change. The facts you keep, the facts you drop, and the first sentence do.

Read `brand/icps.md` before you write. If the prompt does not name an audience, use **First-time visitor**. That is who the welcome email is for.

## How to choose the ICP

| If the booking or prompt is about | Use this ICP |
|---|---|
| A new customer, a welcome email, a first sailing, someone who has not crossed with us | First-time visitor |
| Someone who lives here, commutes, sails weekly, or already knows the boats | Resident commuter |
| A car, van, high-sided vehicle, or the vehicle lane | Vehicle traveler |
| A charter, Little Ossian, a private hire, a group booking of the vessel | Charter booker |
| A wheelchair, assistance dog, hidden disability, or "if you need a hand" as the reason for the letter | Assisted traveler |

If two apply, keep both jobs in one letter. A first-time visitor bringing a car is still a first-time visitor, with the vehicle times in the body. Do not send two emails.

Do not invent a sixth audience. Do not write for "travelers" or "guests".

## What every letter still does

The ICP changes the emphasis. It does not change the company.

- Open with the fact.
- Name the vessel, the terminal, the time, and the date when you have them.
- Sign off `See you on the quay`.
- Hull Navy header, Signal Yellow button, Georgia body. See `brand/design-system.md`.
- Never delighted, journey, family, or an exclamation mark.

## What the ICP changes

| | First-time visitor | Resident commuter | Vehicle traveler | Charter booker | Assisted traveler |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject names | First sailing | The crossing and the time | The vehicle and the sailing | The charter | The arrangement, not the disability |
| Primer on check-in | Yes | No | Vehicle times first | Charter check-in only | Assistance first, then the sailing |
| Weather paragraph | Yes | One line if winter | Yes, including the vehicle deck | Charter cancel terms, not the fare table | Only if it affects the arrangement |
| Word for the reader | Passenger | Passenger | Passenger | Customer | Passenger |
| Typical length | 180 to 220 words | 80 to 120 words | 140 to 180 words | 160 to 200 words | 160 to 200 words |

## Default for a welcome email

`emails/welcome.md` is the first-time visitor letter. If someone asks only for "a welcome email for new customers", write that letter, in that layout, for that ICP. Do not average the five profiles into one.
