# Facts a customer letter may use

These are the public particulars. They are enough to write a welcome, a reminder, or a cancellation. They are not the operations handbook.

## The company

Harborline Ferries runs six vessels from four terminals. The office is at Kelsall Quay.

| Terminal | Usual work |
|---|---|
| Kelsall Quay | Outer crossings, night berths, the company office |
| Nethergill | Outer crossings |
| Tolquin Pier | Estuary service. No overnight berthing. |
| Braewick Slip | Estuary service, Class B and C only |

| Vessel | Class | Notes a passenger might need |
|---|---|---|
| Ardmore Rose | A | Outer crossings. Lift. Vehicle deck. |
| Kelsall Endeavour | A | Outer crossings. Lift. Vehicle deck. |
| Marnoch Star | A | Outer crossings. Lift. Vehicle deck. |
| Tolquin Spirit | B | Estuary. Lift. Vehicle deck. |
| Braewick Trader | B | Estuary. Lift. Vehicle deck. |
| Little Ossian | C | Charter and relief. No vehicle deck. No lift. |

Class A works the outer crossings. Class B works the estuary. Little Ossian is kept for charter and relief.

## On the day

| | Class A (outer) | Class B (estuary) | Little Ossian (charter) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle check-in closes | 25 minutes before | 15 minutes before | No vehicles |
| Foot passenger check-in closes | 15 minutes before | 10 minutes before | 20 minutes before |

Ask passengers to arrive a little earlier than that so they are not walking up as the desk closes. A useful line is: foot passengers 20 minutes before, vehicles 30 minutes before.

A passenger who arrives after check-in has closed is not carried on that sailing. They are rebooked onto the next one at no charge.

From 1 December, terminal waiting rooms open 90 minutes before the first departure. If a sailing is delayed beyond 45 minutes, hot drinks are provided at no charge.

## Assistance

Assisted travel can be booked up to 24 hours before departure. A passenger who arrives without having booked is still carried and still helped. Notice guarantees the arrangement. It does not ration it.

- Wheelchair access to the passenger deck on every Class A and Class B vessel. Not on Little Ossian.
- Assistance dogs on every vessel, at no charge. Tell the crew at check-in.
- A discreet request card is at every desk and with any crew member. No explanation is asked for.
- A wheelchair booking that has been placed on Little Ossian is moved to a Class B vessel. We tell the passenger.

## When things go wrong

Scheduled services: there is no weather exclusion. A canceled sailing, with no alternative the same day, is a full refund of the fare plus £30 per passenger. Delays of 30 minutes or more can be claimed on the online form within 28 days.

| Delay on arrival | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Under 30 minutes | None |
| 30 to 59 minutes | 25 percent of the fare |
| 60 to 119 minutes | 50 percent of the fare |
| 120 minutes or more | 100 percent of the fare |
| Canceled, no alternative same day | 100 percent plus £30 per passenger |

Compensation is paid back to the original method of payment within 14 days of a valid claim.

Charter bookings are a different regime. Do not apply the table above to a charter.

| Canceled by | Notice | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| The charterer | More than 21 days | 90 percent of the fee |
| The charterer | 8 to 21 days | 50 percent of the fee |
| The charterer | 7 days or less | No refund |
| Harborline, weather | Any | 100 percent of the fee, no extra payment |
| Harborline, technical | Any | 100 percent plus 15 percent of the fee as a credit |

A welcome letter does not recite the refund table. Point to it only when the letter is about a delay or a cancellation.
