On the day

Foot passengers: be at the check-in desk 20 minutes before departure. Check-in closes 15 minutes before on the outer crossings and 10 minutes before on the estuary boats.

Vehicles: join the lane 30 minutes before. Vehicle check-in closes 25 minutes before on Class A vessels and 15 minutes before on Class B.

If you arrive after check-in has closed, we will put you on the next sailing at no charge.

If you need a hand

Assisted travel can be arranged up to 24 hours before you sail. If you arrive without having booked, we still carry you and we still help. Ask at any check-in desk for a discreet request card. No explanation is asked for.

Weather

We sail when it is safe and we do not sail when it is not. If we cancel a scheduled service, you get your fare back. There is no weather exclusion. We will write as soon as the decision is made.
