# New customer welcome email

Use this letter whenever someone asks to create, draft, or write a welcome email for new Harborline passengers. Do not write a generic hospitality letter and do not invent a second layout.

This letter is for the **first-time visitor** in `brand/icps.md`. Read `brand/audience.md` first. If the prompt names a commuter, a vehicle, a charter, or assistance, do not send this letter unchanged. Keep the Hull Navy header and Signal Yellow button, and rewrite the body for that ICP.

Follow `brand/voice.md` for the words and `brand/design-system.md` for the colors. The HTML to send is `emails/welcome.html`.

## When this letter goes out

After the first booking is confirmed. One letter, one purpose. It does not sell a day-return and it does not recap the whole refund table.

## Subject and preview

- Subject: `Your first sailing with Harborline`
- Preview: `Your booking is confirmed. Here is what to do on the day.`

Never: "Welcome aboard!" or "We're excited to have you".

## The letter

Subject: Your first sailing with Harborline

Hello {first_name},

Your booking is confirmed. We will see you on the quay.

You are booked on the {route} crossing aboard {vessel}, leaving {terminal} at {time} on {date}.

**On the day**

- Foot passengers: be at the check-in desk 20 minutes before departure. Check-in closes 15 minutes before on the outer crossings and 10 minutes before on the estuary boats.
- Vehicles: join the lane 30 minutes before. Vehicle check-in closes 25 minutes before on Class A vessels and 15 minutes before on Class B.
- If you arrive after check-in has closed, we will put you on the next sailing at no charge.

**If you need a hand**

Assisted travel can be arranged up to 24 hours before you sail. If you arrive without having booked, we still carry you and we still help. Ask at any check-in desk for a discreet request card. No explanation is asked for.

**Weather**

We sail when it is safe and we do not sail when it is not. If we cancel a scheduled service, you get your fare back. There is no weather exclusion. We will write as soon as the decision is made.

See you on the quay,

Harborline Ferries
Kelsall Quay

Button: `View your booking`, Signal Yellow, Hull Navy label.

## What this letter is not

It is not a coupon, a survey, or a brand manifesto. It does not use Boot Topping Red. It does not put a photograph of a ferry behind the header. It does not say "welcome to the family".
